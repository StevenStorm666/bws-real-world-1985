Known gaps and errors in the database, roughly by importance.

## People and records
- Missing pros of 1 January 1985: Suh Sung-in (IBF super bantamweight champion until 3 Jan 1985), Ring top-10 contenders
  Don Lee, Henry Hearns, Pete McIntyre, Candido Tellez and Carlos Trujillo, and Genaro Hernandez, John David Jackson,
  Vincent Pettway, Robert Daniels, Steve Little and Tomas Molinares.
- Missing debutants: Lomachenko and Anthony Joshua (2013), and world champions Leavander Johnson, Daisuke Naito,
  Yodsanan Sor Nanthachai, Julio Pablo Chacón, Maselino Masoe, Daorung Chuvatana, Frankie Toledo, José Bonilla,
  Isidro García and Mauricio Martínez.
- Missing amateurs: An Young-su, Lee Jae-hyuk, Timofey Skryabin, Chris Sande, Grahame Cheney, Juan Carlos Lemus,
  Daniel Dumitrescu, Héctor López. Some debutants were still amateurs in 1985 (e.g. Henry Wharton, Nicky Piper, Michael
  Ayers, Eyüp Can) and should move to the amateurs; 83 amateur records are estimates.
- Records that don't match: Lee Dong-chun, Jean-Marie Emebe, Elmer Magallano, Eusebio Espinal, Celso Chávez, Chisanda
  Mutti. Horacio Saldano and Juan Domingo Suárez may no longer have been active in 1985.
- Debuts: about 100 birth years are estimates; Sebastián Luján debuted around 2000, not 2003; Bandon Sitbangprachan may
  have boxed pro before 1985; Brian Holmes and Neil Munn are rated far above their real careers.
- Wilfredo Gómez and Juan LaPorte were featherweights on 1 Jan 1985 (kept at super featherweight, where they won their
  later titles).
- Cus D'Amato stays active after his death in November 1985.

## Titles
- No Commonwealth titles; British titles only in six divisions.

## Trainers, gyms, officials
- 478 debutants have a trainer who was working in 1985 instead of the one they really had later (e.g. Freddie Roach),
  because the game cannot add trainers during play.
- Many trainers' ages and gyms are unconfirmed; some are at the wrong gym (e.g. Ulli Wegner, Manfred Wolke).
- Promoters: Kyoei's head was banned 1982-89; Big Fights Inc. was a management company; 318 non-journeymen have no
  known 1985 promoter.

## World
- 76 venues did not exist yet in 1985 (e.g. MGM Grand Garden Arena, Tokyo Dome); the game has no opening date for venues.
- Some texts mention later events: the Showtime, ITV and BBC histories, the AIBA description, 18 sponsor descriptions
  and the four anti-doping agencies (all founded after 1985).
- USBA founding year (1976-77, not 1981); Cardiff Arms Park capacity.
- 12 East German amateurs are treated as West German by the game (it only shows East Germany for birthplaces Leipzig
  and Dresden), so they can turn pro before 1990.
- Nations the game does not know (Denmark, Jamaica and others) show a grey flag and no region.
- Thin divisions: super middleweight and super flyweight.
